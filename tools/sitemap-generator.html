PRACTICAL GUIDE

Sitemap Generator

Turn a clean list of absolute URLs into an XML sitemap you can inspect and publish.

What it checks

A sitemap is most useful when its URLs represent the site's intended canonical, indexable pages.

How to use the result

Remove temporary, duplicate, redirected, or non-canonical URLs before deployment. The generator creates XML from the URL list; it does not crawl or verify the targets.
