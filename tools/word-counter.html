Web Text Counter

Measure words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time.

What it checks

Text counts are useful when planning articles, landing pages, product descriptions, and editorial updates.

How to use the result

Use the numbers as planning signals rather than quality targets. Clear writing, useful coverage, and audience fit matter more than reaching a particular word count.
