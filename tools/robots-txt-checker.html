PRACTICAL GUIDE

Robots.txt Checker

Inspect crawler directives and look for rules that deserve a second look.

What it checks

robots.txt is a crawler control file, not a general privacy mechanism. A broad Disallow rule can prevent crawlers from fetching resources or pages you intended to expose.

How to use the result

Review the user-agent groups, blocked paths, and sitemap references before publishing changes. Never use robots.txt as the only mechanism for sensitive information.
