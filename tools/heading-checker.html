PRACTICAL GUIDE

Heading Checker

See the H1–H6 structure of a page at a glance.

What it checks

Headings help readers and assistive technology understand document structure, while a clear hierarchy also makes long pages easier to maintain.

How to use the result

Look for a meaningful main heading, sensible section nesting, and headings that describe real sections. Do not add headings merely to hit a target count.
