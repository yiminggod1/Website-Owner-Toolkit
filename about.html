ABOUT

Practical tools for the person who owns the site.

Website Owner Toolkit focuses on specific website jobs: check a signal, understand the finding, and move to the next fix.

Design direction

This project intentionally avoids the repeated “AI dashboard” look used by many generic tool sites.

It uses an editorial utility style: warm paper tones, thin rules, serif display type, restrained color, and tool pages that feel like reference sheets rather than a wall of glowing cards.

The site is designed to grow by search intent, not by endlessly adding unrelated widgets.

Built around jobs

Meta tags, headings, social cards, sitemaps, schema, redirects, DNS, campaign URLs, and other concrete website-owner tasks.

Technology and references

The implementation is independent. Product and architecture research included mature open-source website auditing projects such as Trivium, Tanaguru, CrawlScope, and focused meta-tag checkers.

Cross-origin browser limitations are surfaced instead of hidden: the URL audit can fall back to pasted HTML when a target site blocks direct browser access.
