PRIVACY

How the toolkit handles information.

Most checks are designed to run in your browser, with a small number of features making external requests that are necessary to return a result.

Browser processing

HTML, XML, text, headers, and generator fields are processed in your browser for the relevant tools. The site does not require an account or ask you to create a profile.

External requests

The DNS Lookup and Email DNS Checker send DNS-over-HTTPS requests to the public Cloudflare DNS resolver you are querying. The Website Audit tool may request a URL you enter when the target page permits a browser cross-origin read. When that is blocked, the site uses the pasted-source fallback instead of attempting to bypass browser security controls.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current repository contains reserved advertisement areas but does not yet embed an advertising provider. When Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, this policy will be updated to identify the vendors in use and describe the applicable cookies, local storage, data processing, and user choices.

Google states that third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other sites. Google also provides Ads Settings as an option for users to manage personalized advertising. Other vendors or ad networks may have their own policies and opt-out mechanisms.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to use an appropriate certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. The final advertising implementation will be configured through the applicable AdSense privacy and consent settings before ads are activated.

When third-party services are enabled, those providers may process technical information such as IP address, device/browser information, approximate location, or interaction data according to their own policies and the configuration used on this site.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality, analytics, advertising, or data flows change. The published version on this page is the applicable version for visitors.

Data minimization

The toolkit is intentionally browser-first for many operations, so ordinary checks can often be completed without sending page content to a server operated by this site.
