PRIVACY

Your inputs should stay understandable.

These browser-first tools are designed to process user-provided data in the browser wherever practical.

What is processed

Text, HTML, XML, headers, and generator fields are processed in your browser for the focused tools. The DNS utility sends a DNS-over-HTTPS query to the selected public resolver because a DNS lookup requires a resolver.

The Website Audit page may attempt a browser fetch of a URL you enter. If that fetch is blocked by browser cross-origin policy, the tool does not bypass the restriction; it asks you to paste the page source instead.

Advertising

Advertisement placeholders are reserved in the design. When an advertising provider is added, its own privacy and cookie policies may apply.
